# YouTube Shorts / Reel Script: The Dollar Trade

**Target Duration:** ~100 seconds
**Pacing:** Fast, engaging, high energy
**Visuals/B-roll Suggestions Included**

---

**(0:00 - 0:08) Hook**
**Host (On Camera, holding a physical dollar bill):**
If you’re an NRI, India is practically *begging* for your dollars right now. And they’re willing to pay you an insane 6 to 7% interest—*in dollars*—to get them. 

**(0:08 - 0:20) The Setup**
**Host (On Camera):**
It’s called an FCNR deposit. Normally, keeping your money in US dollars might earn you 4%. 
**Visual:** *Split screen showing US bank (4%) vs Indian bank (6.5%).*
**Host (Voiceover):**
But Indian banks are saying: "Give us your dollars, we’ll keep them as dollars, and pay you 6.5%." No rupee conversion. No currency risk.

**(0:20 - 0:40) The Secret Arbitrage**
**Host (On Camera, leaning in closely):**
But here’s where the smart money is playing a completely different game. It’s not a fixed deposit—it’s a subsidized cross-border carry trade. 
**Visual:** *Text on screen: THE DOLLAR TRADE.*
**Host (Voiceover):**
Savvy investors are *borrowing* dollars at 4%, putting them in this FCNR deposit at 6.5%, and pocketing the difference! 
**Visual:** *Graphics showing leveraging: $100k capital + $400k borrowed -> 16%+ Return on Equity.*
**Host (On Camera):**
If you use leverage, your Return on Equity can shoot up from 6.5% to over 16%!

**(0:40 - 1:00) The "Why"**
**Host (On Camera, serious tone):**
But *why* is the Reserve Bank of India giving away free money? 
**Visual:** *Graphic of the RBI building, money printing press with Rupee symbol, then showing an empty Dollar printing press.*
**Host (Voiceover):**
Because the RBI can print rupees all day long, but they *cannot* print dollars. And India needs dollars. Badly. 

**(1:00 - 1:15) The Machine**
**Host (On Camera):**
So, the RBI engineered a massive subsidy. They lowered the currency hedging costs for Indian banks so they could offer these crazy rates to you. 
**Visual:** *Counter racking up to $52.3 Billion fast.*
**Host (Voiceover):**
And it worked. In just weeks, over $52 Billion flooded in. 

**(1:15 - 1:30) The Warning**
**Host (On Camera, cautioning):**
But remember the golden rule of finance: *A yield is never just a number. It’s the price of taking a risk.* 
**Visual:** *Clock ticking forward to 2029.*
**Host (Voiceover):**
The RBI is just buying time. Fast forward to 2029 when these deposits mature. The special rates will probably be gone. 

**(1:30 - 1:40) Outro**
**Host (On Camera):**
The question is: will you keep your dollars in India, or take them back? 
**Visual:** *Call to action on screen: LIKE & SUBSCRIBE.*
**Host:**
Would you take the Dollar Trade? Let me know in the comments!
