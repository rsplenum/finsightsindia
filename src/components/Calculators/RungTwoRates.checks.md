# Design checks — RungTwoRates (rung 1)

**Question:** *So how fast is your money really growing?*

| entry | check | answer |
|---|---|---|
| dd-002 | one question, ≤2 new concepts? | One question. One concept — that rising prices eat into growth — and it is taught here because everything downstream depends on it. |
| dd-003 | what do they leave with? | The comparison the whole subject reduces to: what the pot really grows at, against what they are really taking out. Usable on any product we never built. |
| dd-004 | concept not yet given? | This is where real-vs-nominal is *taught*, in the reader's own words, without either term. |
| dd-005 | the one comparison? | Yes — this rung exists to establish it. Tax, crashes and hedging all arrive later as pushes on these two numbers. |
| dd-006 | anything to diff? | No controls at all. |
| dd-009 | any unexplained figure? | Real growth is derived on screen from two numbers already shown. Nothing arrives unannounced. |

**Deliberate choices**
- Real growth is the **ratio**, not the difference: 12% against 6% leaves 5.66%, and treating it as 6% compounds badly over thirty years.
- The handoff names the three things that eat the margin, so each later rung is expected rather than a surprise.
