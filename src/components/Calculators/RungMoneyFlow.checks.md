# Design checks — RungMoneyFlow (rung 2)

**Question:** *Where does the money actually go?*

| entry | check | answer |
|---|---|---|
| dd-002 | one question, ≤2 new concepts? | One question, **zero** new concepts — rung 1 already taught that prices rise; this shows it in rupees. |
| dd-003 | what do they leave with? | That the rate they draw matters more than the amount they start with. |
| dd-004 | which money is each figure in? | Column headers, permanent, never hunted for. |
| dd-006 | anything to diff? | **This is the entry's origin.** A toggle here was removed: both moneys are now adjacent, because the gap between them is the lesson and a lesson cannot be a mode. |
| dd-007 | does every visual difference assert something? | One prose voice at one size. Emphasis only on the total row and the inflation line — the two things that matter. |
| dd-010 | lived experience or accountant's summary? | Flow over thirty years, which is what a person experiences as spending, not a terminal figure. |

**Deliberate choices**
- The total counts what reached *the reader* — spending plus what they still hold. Tax went to the government, so it is shown but not counted, and the three rows above must add to the row below because a reader will check.
