# Design checks — RetirementAnswer (Layer 1, the Answer)

**Question this screen answers:** *Will my savings last?*

| entry | check | answer |
|---|---|---|
| dd-002 | one question, ≤2 new concepts? | One. Three inputs, no new concepts — savings, monthly need, years are all things the visitor arrived knowing. |
| dd-003 | what do they leave with? | That the answer is a date and an amount, not a probability — and that a bad answer comes with levers, not just a verdict. |
| dd-004 | can they say which money each figure is in? | Yes — "in today's prices" is stated in the sentence itself, not a caption. |
| dd-005 | pushes on the spine? | It *is* the spine's output. The two rates that explain it come next. |
| dd-006 | anything to diff? | No modes, no toggles. |
| dd-007 | does every visual difference assert something? | Headline vs detail: the headline is the answer, the detail is its qualification. Remedy cards are equal weight because no lever is recommended over another. |

**Deliberate choices**
- Assumptions sit under the answer in plain words, never a tooltip (sol-019).
- Remedies are hidden entirely when the plan works — offering fixes to someone with no problem invents one.
