# Content Quality & Narrative Structure Checklist

Before publishing any article, you MUST verify it passes this 100% Gold Standard checklist.

## 1. The 4-Pillar Psychological Narrative
Does the article structure directly mirror the user's anxious internal monologue?
- [ ] **Pillar 1: What is the regulation?** (Explaining the exact legal mechanics and sections with zero fluff).
- [ ] **Pillar 2: How can I minimize my risk?** (Using `<NoticeTrap>` components to highlight CPC scrutiny triggers and compliance deadlines).
- [ ] **Pillar 3: How can I save money?** (Detailing exemptions, deductions, and loopholes, often paired with a `<TaxWorkbench>` interactive calculator).
- [ ] **Pillar 4: Am I leaving anything out?** (The 360-degree Edge Cases—exploring highly obscure, specific nuances that generic tax sites ignore).

## 2. Depth and "Meat"
- [ ] Does the article contain exhaustive, real-world examples (e.g., Joint Home Loans, NRI status impact, 1.25L marginal mechanics)?
- [ ] Are the explanations rich in prose, prioritizing extreme clarity over brevity?

## 3. The Final Actionable Checklist
- [ ] Does the article conclude with a `## 💡 Key Takeaway Summary & Actionable Checklist`?
- [ ] Does this checklist give the user concrete, physical next steps (e.g., "Draft a rent agreement", "File Form 10-IEA before July 31st")?

## 4. Tone and Empathy
- [ ] Is the tone empathetic to the user's friction/problem?
- [ ] Is it highly technical when showing the math?
- [ ] Is it appropriately cautionary when explaining the Notice Traps?
- [ ] Does it make the user feel elevated and satisfied, never lesser for not knowing technical jargon?
