# FinSight Content Standards: The Video-First Architecture

## 1. The Strategy
Humans are visual learners. Reading complex tax code is a forced habit, but listening to stories comes naturally. 
Therefore, every article on FinSight is not just an article—it is a pre-written, highly potent script for short-form video distribution (YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels).

## 2. The 100-Second Standard
Every topic must inherently possess the "masala" required for a fast-paced, 150-word video script.
Even if an article is 1,200 words deep, it must be architected so the core paradox can be summarized visually in 100 seconds.

## 3. The Required Script Block
Going forward, every new `.mdx` article MUST contain a final section explicitly titled: **"The 100-Second Reel"** (or styled equivalently to the article's tone).

This block MUST follow the **4-Step Rapid Sequential Script Format**:

1. **The Hook (The Paradox):** A contradictory or anxiety-inducing question that stops scrolling. *(e.g., "Why are you paying 30% tax on crypto even if you lost all your money?")*
2. **The Anxiety (The Trap):** State the brutal truth of the law without sugarcoating it.
3. **The Pivot (The Strategy):** The specific legal loophole, deduction, or action step to take.
4. **The Relief (The Call to Action):** The mathematical outcome and a push to use the FinSight calculators or read the master guide.

*This structure exactly mirrors our Anxiety & Relief visual illustration guidelines.*
