Deferred to T4

Portfolio insurance pays instead of costing sol-018

Hedging in the SIP engine more than doubles the median outcome, inverting the site’s own claim that puts cost 1.5–2.2% a year. Cause: an annual −8% floor divided by 12 and applied monthly — a floor is not a rate. It truncates losses in 35.8% of months against the ~10% of years a real floor would bind.

Waiting on: T4. Fixing it means tracking the equity sleeve separately so the floor lands at year end — a redesign of the simulation loop, not a repair. Recorded as a deliberate it.fails test so the suite stays green and turns red when fixed.

Deferred to T5

The insurance analyser’s core logic is untestable where it sits

runReplicationAnalysis() — the unbundling comparison that is the tool’s whole value proposition — lives inline in a 35KB page. Its XIRR and Black-Scholes dependencies are tested; this is not.

Waiting on: T5, which starts with the extraction before any UI work.

Deferred — needs coverage first

Rule retirements in the content factory

The relaxation operator flags 11 of 17 evaluator rules as never having fired, but at 5.9% logging coverage that is a measurement artefact, not a finding — it would have recommended deleting R7 and M4, both added days ago from real failures.

Waiting on: a decision on whether it is its own rung or part of the sequence rung. Recorded so the idea does not evaporate — Rahul: "this is only my musing, but this feature, i think, could add more value."
